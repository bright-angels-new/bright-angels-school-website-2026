WHO WE ARE

A place to learn, belong and become.

Bright Angels Junior School is a learning community in Lukwanga, Wakiso District. We aim to nurture curious minds, good character and a love of learning in every child.

Our school works hand in hand with parents and guardians to support learners from the early years through primary school.

OUR LEARNING JOURNEY

Every stage matters.

From the early years to the end of primary school, each class is an important step in a child's growth.

01

Baby Class

A gentle introduction to learning through play, discovery and guided activities.

02

Middle Class

Building communication, curiosity and early learning foundations.

03

Top Class

Strengthening school readiness, confidence and foundational skills.

04

Primary 1–3

Developing essential literacy, numeracy and learning habits.

05

Primary 4–5

Growing understanding, independence and subject knowledge.

06

Primary 6–7

Building confidence and preparing for the next stage of education.
