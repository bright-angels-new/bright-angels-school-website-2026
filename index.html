WHO WE ARE

A place to learn, belong and thrive.

Bright Angels Junior School is committed to providing children with a supportive environment where every learner is encouraged to do their best.

We value strong foundations in learning, good character, respect, curiosity and partnership with parents.

Our promise

To guide every child with care, encourage a love of learning and help each learner discover their potential.
